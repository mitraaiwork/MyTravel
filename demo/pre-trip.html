Visit Japan Web — Action Required Complete online arrival registration to skip queues at immigration
⚠ Do this now

Japan's Visit Japan Web lets you pre-register your arrival declaration, fast-track immigration, and set up the Japan Duty Free QR code — all in one place. It takes 15 minutes and saves up to 45 minutes in the arrivals queue.
